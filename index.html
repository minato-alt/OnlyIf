And I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the times I made things more difficult for you when I knew you already had so much on your plate. I'm sorry for the moments when my feelings, expectations or the way I handled things added to what you were already dealing with. I never wanted to become another thing you had to worry about. I know I couldn't always understand everything you were going through, and perhaps I didn't always make things easier either. I wish I had been better at that.

One last thing...

If there's one thing I hope you remember.

I'm sorry I couldn't make the weight any lighter for you. I wish I could have been someone who made even the difficult days a little easier, rather than sometimes adding to them.

But please don't worry about me. I'll be okay. I want you to focus on yourself, your happiness and the goals you've always dreamed of achieving. I hope you get there, and I hope the journey is kinder to you than you expect.

And if somewhere along the way our paths become distant, I hope you never let that stop you from being happy. I hope you find everything you've been looking for, experience all the things you've been wishing for, and have someone beside you who makes you feel loved and appreciated every single day.

I don't know what the future holds, but I know I'll remember you, and I'll always wish the very best for you.

Thank you for these nine months.

Take care of yourself, cupcake.
